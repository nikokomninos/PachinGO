#### Changes
- Levels can now have their names and descriptions edited after upload, via a
button in the level's popup.
- Users can now upload profile pictures!
- A random level can now be selected on the search page

#### Fixes
- Usernames can no longer be empty or contain spaces and special characters. All
usernames must now be between 5 and 30 characters. Users who had previously
registered with usernames shorter than this will get to keep their names.
